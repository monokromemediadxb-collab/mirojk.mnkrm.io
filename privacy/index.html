7. How we protect it

  • Enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the website owner can open.
  • The form sends data over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own server, which checks it before it is saved.
  • The access keys that connect the form to the sheet are kept as encrypted secrets, not in the website code.
  • The website uses a strict security policy and security headers to limit injection and tampering attacks.
  • Access to our accounts is protected with two-step verification.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the UAE Data Office as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPL you can ask us, free of charge, to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct data that is wrong or complete data that is missing;
  • delete your data where we no longer need it or you withdraw your consent;
  • restrict or stop how we use your data, or object to its use;
  • give you your data in a portable format, or transfer it to another provider where technically possible;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew.

To use any of these rights, email hello@monokrome.com. We will respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UAE Data Office, the federal authority that oversees data protection.

10. Children

This website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

We may update this policy as our website or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
